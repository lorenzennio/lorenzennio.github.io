<p>The official Belle II collaboration paper releasing a public, model-agnostic likelihood for the \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) measurement, built with the general method described above. It lets anyone test their own new-physics model against the measurement's data directly, without access to the internal analysis.</p>

<p>Published as: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/pr66-sd36">Model-agnostic likelihood for the reinterpretation of the \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) measurement at Belle II</a>, Phys. Rev. D 112, 092016 (2025).</p>

<center><img src="/assets/images/knunu-combination-samples.png" width="70%"/></center>

<p>Posterior samples on the effective Wilson coefficients from combining the public likelihood with a projected future dataset (50 ab\(^{-1}\)): constraints sharpen substantially with more data, resolving degeneracies present in today's 362 fb\(^{-1}\) result.</p>
