REDIST is a reweighting method for reinterpreting binned analyses in high-energy physics: it recomputes how an observable's distribution changes under alterations to the kinematic distributions of a decay channel. It's built on [pyhf](https://github.com/scikit-hep/pyhf) for statistical inference, with an optional JAX backend for differentiable likelihood calculations, and integrates with [EOS](https://eos.github.io/) for theoretical predictions. The method is described in [arXiv:2402.08417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08417).
