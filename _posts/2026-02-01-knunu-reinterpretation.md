<p>A line of work on reinterpreting the Belle II \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) measurement without redoing the full experimental analysis for every new-physics model: a model-agnostic likelihood, built directly from the measured signal region yields, that lets anyone test their own model against the data by supplying only its predicted event distribution.</p>

<ul>
  <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-024-13038-4">Constructing model-agnostic likelihoods, a method for the reinterpretation of particle physics results</a> &mdash; the general method, introduced and validated on simulated data.</li>
  <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/pr66-sd36">Model-agnostic likelihood for the reinterpretation of the \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) measurement at Belle II</a> &mdash; the official Belle II collaboration paper releasing the public likelihood for the \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) analysis.</li>
  <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/ggtk-gkx4">Constraints on invisible \(B^+\to K^+X\) decays from the Belle II \(B^+\to K^+\nu\bar\nu\) measurement</a> &mdash; applying that likelihood to constrain a generic invisible particle \(X\), including a heavy dark-sector mediator interpretation.</li>
</ul>
